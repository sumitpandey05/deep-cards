# Deep Cards — Question Dataset

> **How to use this file:**
> - Add your questions under the correct section.
> - Each level shows exactly 6 questions.
> - For every level, 3 questions come from `GENERAL` and 3 come from the matched combo section.
> - The 3 general questions are randomized each time a fresh deck is requested.
> - Combo-specific questions are shown only when both age group AND genders match.
> - Keep at least 3 combo-specific questions per level for every combo, and add more whenever you want extra variety.
> - Add more general questions freely to increase the random pool for every combination.
